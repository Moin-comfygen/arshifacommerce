Product360 Research Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Product360 Research is a local-first Chrome extension that helps a user research a Flipkart product page they choose to analyze.

Information handled

When the extension popup is opened on Flipkart, Product360 reads product information visible on the active page, which may include the page URL, product title, image, price, rating and rating/review counts, accessible review excerpts, and product listings already present on that page. It does not read passwords, payment information, cookies, or private account information.

How information is used

The page information is processed in the browser to create the requested product report, calculate local scores and clearly labeled estimates, and save local research history. A user may also enter costs in the optional profit calculator; these inputs are used only for that on-device calculation.

Storage, sharing, and retention

Reports and history are saved in the extension's local IndexedDB database. A small preference and the identifier of the latest report are saved in Chrome's local extension storage. Product360 does not operate a report backend, transmit report data to its publisher, or share the report data with third parties. Older report, audit, and snapshot records are limited by local retention settings. Removing the extension removes its local extension data.

Permissions

  • Storage is used to save local reports and preferences.
  • Flipkart site access is used only to read visible content on Flipkart pages for the product research feature.

Product360 does not access unrelated browsing history, cookies, or hidden/private marketplace endpoints and does not bypass authentication, CAPTCHA, or rate limits.

Security and changes

Product360 is designed to keep reports on the user's device. This policy may be updated if the extension's behavior changes. The policy's revision date will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the publisher using the support contact shown on the Product360 Research Chrome Web Store listing or the publisher-controlled support link provided there.
